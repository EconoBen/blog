## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Diagnose the visible proportion problem

The revision SHALL identify the source of the small/narrow opening silhouette from actual baseline playback, adjacent frames and authentic grebe references before selecting the correction. It SHALL distinguish observed reference anatomy from animation choices and SHALL preserve prior failed comparisons as history.

#### Scenario: Baseline diagnosis

- **WHEN** the implementation studies the current opening and prior enlargement experiment
- **THEN** it records how joint opening, feather area, near/far projection and closed-wing material timing contribute to the complaint, with direct evidence rather than treating a larger numeric span as sufficient

### Requirement: Full wings unfold through connected anatomy

The bird SHALL retain full-length wing segments during unfolding and present visibly substantial feather area early in the opening, appropriate to its compact grebe body. Shoulder, elbow, wrist and primary-feather motion SHALL remain connected; near/far views SHALL be distinct and proportionate. It SHALL NOT replace folding with miniature growth, a narrow rigid panel, duplicated closed-wing paint, a wingless interval or an abrupt material/silhouette switch.

#### Scenario: Early emergence through first strokes

- **WHEN** the opening is watched from its resting wing through deployment and two complete powered cycles
- **THEN** the visible silhouette reads as the same full wing folding out from the shoulder, with credible fullness before the first stroke and continuous changes of elbow, wrist and feathers

#### Scenario: Reference-informed proportions

- **WHEN** near and far wings pass through raised, extended, edge-on and recovery poses
- **THEN** projection and feather area remain believable relative to the body without obscuring the head or enlarging the grebe into another bird's silhouette

### Requirement: Independent isolated visual acceptance precedes integration

The implementers SHALL compare a bounded set of materially different studies and record the selected approach's visual advantage. Independent desktop and phone reviews SHALL inspect normal-speed unfolding plus two complete wingbeats and native adjacent frames before the correction enters the full opening. Numeric tests SHALL NOT be accepted as proof of visual success.

#### Scenario: Choosing the study

- **WHEN** two initial candidates have been recorded under equivalent framing and timing
- **THEN** an independent critic identifies the stronger candidate and any remaining must-fix defects; at most one materially different follow-up study is added before reassessing the diagnosis or artwork

#### Scenario: Visual gate fails

- **WHEN** the reviewer still observes thin emergence, implausible proportions, duplicate/absent wing material or discontinuity
- **THEN** integration remains unaccepted, failed evidence is retained, and the next revision addresses the concrete visual finding rather than merely relaxing a geometry threshold

### Requirement: Preserve the complete two-pass film

The correction SHALL preserve the 17.6-second shared clock, scene/wake/shake/feather/ripple beats, two distinct left-to-right passes, actual canonical Agent Memory presentation, 10000–13300 ms quiet hold, second-pass removal/site reveal and continuous single-resident landing. The moderate connected neck/body, level recognizable bill and exact resting handoff SHALL remain intact. A directly necessary deployment-channel or wing-compositing change SHALL NOT reset or retime unrelated beats.

#### Scenario: Integrated opening

- **WHEN** the complete desktop or phone film plays
- **THEN** revised wings remain convincing at the hero, both crossing scales and landing, the cover remains separated from the beak/feet/wings, and one bird reaches the existing resident without clipping, duplication, art jump or new scroll correction

### Requirement: Preserve access and restrained pond behavior

The revision SHALL retain native preparation and graceful resource failure, Skip/Escape, replay, reduced-motion bypass, once-per-session/history/hash rules, hidden-time preservation, focus/scroll restoration and cleanup. It SHALL remain silent and SHALL preserve the original two-slot swimmer cadence, initial swimmer, peeker and functional resident interaction.

#### Scenario: Interruption or failed revised resource

- **WHEN** the film is skipped, navigation occurs, motion preference changes, or any essential artwork fails or misses the deadline
- **THEN** the site becomes usable without transient book/bird/locks or late restart, and ordinary pond behavior resumes within its existing rules

### Requirement: Bounded rendering with honest validation

The revision SHALL preserve once-per-actor preparation, cached static coverage, shared reflection, hidden-work guards, bounded Canvas allocation and disposal. It SHALL use meaningful failing regressions for changed geometry/material contracts and SHALL retain current source/art identity for visual, resource and production evidence. Performance claims SHALL distinguish native scheduling measurements from recordings and physical-device evidence.

#### Scenario: Updated resource and performance evidence

- **WHEN** the candidate is frozen
- **THEN** the complete local source/type/build/served gate passes, resource changes and fresh unrecorded desktop/phone-viewport phase measurements are recorded, and startup or visual limits are explicitly retained

### Requirement: Local verified delivery and fresh shareable video

The completed correction SHALL replace port 3112 only with a verified local production snapshot and SHALL include fresh desktop/phone MP4s from that exact candidate. The MP4s SHALL contain the full opening through landing, play at normal speed and use broadly playable H.264/yuv420p with faststart. Prior MP4s SHALL remain labeled as the rejected-proportion baseline. No push, merge, hosted preview, remote settings mutation or deployment SHALL occur without separate approval.

#### Scenario: Final handoff

- **WHEN** implementation, independent review and final local verification are complete
- **THEN** the user receives the working local preview, current full-opening MP4 paths, exact candidate/evidence identity and concise remaining limits, with nothing published or sent to others
