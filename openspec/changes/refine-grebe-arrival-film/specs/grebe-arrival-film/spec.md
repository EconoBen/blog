## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: A composed natural-history opening
The opening SHALL show a large resting adult horned grebe within a coherent engraved pond, with foreground, open water, distant shore, reflected light and restrained motion. The active opening SHALL fully conceal the website until its intended reveal.

#### Scenario: First eligible homepage arrival
- **WHEN** the assets are ready and the opening begins
- **THEN** the bird and pond form a complete opaque composition without page bleed-through, unintended clipping or background rectangles

### Requirement: Connected waking and takeoff
The grebe SHALL wake through connected eye, head, neck and body movement, shake with staggered anatomical response, anticipate launch, then accelerate vertically above the screen with articulated wings. Contact drops and disturbance SHALL continue after departure.

#### Scenario: Wake through launch playback
- **WHEN** the sequence is viewed at full speed and through adjacent transition frames
- **THEN** posture, wing joints and water response remain connected without rigid whole-image shaking, detached wings, abrupt body substitutions or discontinuous motion

### Requirement: Feather contact and quiet interval
One feather SHALL descend with changing orientation and air resistance, settle onto the pond and create delicate ripples before the returning bird appears.

#### Scenario: Feather reaches water
- **WHEN** the feather’s lower contact point reaches the surface
- **THEN** its motion settles continuously and a small ripple expands from that contact point

### Requirement: Flight reveals and joins the homepage
The grebe SHALL fly left to right, reveal the actual website in coordination with that crossing, then curve and decelerate into the existing pond. Landing SHALL maintain a single readable bird silhouette and match the resident’s position, scale and pose without ghosted bodies or a visible positional jump.

#### Scenario: Crossing through settled landing
- **WHEN** the reveal and landing play on desktop and phone
- **THEN** the flight and reveal remain aligned, contact is visible, the resident handoff is visually continuous and any camera movement has a necessary spatial purpose

### Requirement: Preserve site access and resource bounds
The opening SHALL retain immediate Skip/Escape, accessible focus, reduced motion, once-per-session eligibility, replay, bounded loading, navigation cleanup, silent playback and the original two-slot roaming schedule. Assets SHALL remain local and buffers SHALL remain bounded and released.

#### Scenario: Interrupted or unsupported opening
- **WHEN** a visitor skips, navigates, requests reduced motion or encounters missing assets
- **THEN** the normal website remains usable, state is restored appropriately and animation resources are released

### Requirement: Visual evidence defines readiness
The local candidate SHALL include desktop and phone full-speed recordings, reviewed key frames, independent motion critique, relevant regression/build checks and candid remaining limitations. Technical success alone SHALL NOT count as visual completion.

#### Scenario: Final review candidate
- **WHEN** this round is presented as complete
- **THEN** each story beat and safeguard has current evidence, discovered visual defects have been addressed, and no push, merge, hosted preview or deployment has occurred without approval
